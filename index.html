Dashang Game Rules

🤠 Agent (A): Swaps position with own Rook, Knight, or Bishop. Immune to standard captures (except Pawns & King). Cooldown: 5 turns.

🧱 Democrat (D): Teleports to any empty square. Immune to standard captures (except Pawns & King). Cooldown: 5 turns.

♟️ Pawns: Can capture Agent & Democrat straight forward as well as diagonally.
